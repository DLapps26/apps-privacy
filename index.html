Privacy Policy for DL applications

This document explains how DL applications handles information when you use our apps. By installing or using any of our applications, you agree to the practices described below. We only collect data that is necessary to ensure the proper functioning and improvement of our services, and we never share your information outside the cases described in this policy.

Use of Android applications and personal data

Our applications do not store or transmit any personally identifiable information about you. To help us understand how our apps are used and to improve their stability, we rely on Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. These tools follow Google’s own Privacy Policy. You may disable analytics at any time from the app’s settings if you prefer not to share this information. However, these insights are extremely helpful for us to maintain and enhance the quality of our apps.

Data deletion

As mentioned above, we do not keep any personal data ourselves. Only the third‑party services we use (such as Firebase) may hold anonymized technical information. If you would like us to request the deletion of your anonymized data from these services, you can contact us using the email address available on the app’s Google Play page and include the subject line “please delete my user data”. If the third‑party services accept the request, your anonymized data should then be removed.

Emails

We may contact you by email only in situations where it is necessary for the service (for example, purchase confirmations) or when you have explicitly asked for support. We do not share your email address or any personal information with third parties, except with service providers directly involved in delivering our services as described above.

Online payments

Payments for our products are handled by trusted external providers. These companies may require access to your personal or payment information to process transactions. When you make a purchase through the Google Play Store, Google manages the entire payment process. We never have access to your credit card details. We only share the minimum information required for payment processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted on this page, and we encourage users to review it periodically to stay informed about how we protect their information.
